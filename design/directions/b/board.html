drum.town rebrand · direction board B · designer exploration — not implementation

Count-In

Bold rhythmic graphic. Dark-first: the site is the stage, the score is lit, the accent is a drum-machine LED. Type is Archivo at poster weight; rules are thick like barlines; chapters are tracks and lessons are pads. The metronome stops floating and becomes the transport — a full-width dock that owns the bottom edge of every page. One CSS file, Eleventy, zero client dependencies.

04

The Transport — docked metronome

The metronome stops being furniture in the corner and becomes the transport: a full-width dock fixed to the bottom edge of every page, like a DAW. The page body always reserves the dock's height in padding, so content scrolls above it — overlap is impossible by construction, at 1280px and everywhere else.

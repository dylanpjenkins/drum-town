drum.town rebrand · direction board A · designer exploration — not implementation

The Square

Warm civic modern. drum.town as an actual town square: wayfinding chips instead of dashes, a clock tower instead of a floating widget, a seal instead of a manuscript. Fraunces + Public Sans + IBM Plex Mono. Light-first with a full "evening in the square" dark theme. Everything below ships as one hand-written CSS file on Eleventy.

01

Homepage — hero, primary action, one chapter

A first-time visitor gets walked in the front gate; a returning neighbor is recognized. The chapter list becomes "stops on the map" — a chip grid with zero separators, so nothing orphans at a wrap.

The Single Paradiddle

The most useful sticking pattern ever invented.

⏱15–20 min ◎Rudiment & sticking ♩Practice at 70 → 100 BPM

✓ Walk these first: The Single Stroke Roll · The Double Stroke Roll

1A
Single Paradiddle on Snare
4/4 · 16th notes · ♩ 70 → 100
Electronic ▾

Sticking: R L R R · L R L L. The accents fall on counts 1, 2, 3, 4 — the start of each paradiddle group. Let those be slightly louder than the rest.

Move on when

  • RLRR LRLL × 2 at ♩=80 for 2 minutes without sticking errors
  • Accents on 1, 2, 3, 4 are clearly louder than the rest

04

The Clock Tower — docked metronome

The town keeps one clock, and it sits on the skyline — in the sticky header — not in the street. Collapsed, it is a pill that occupies zero content space. Expanded, it is a user-invoked popover under the header edge, dismissed by Esc, scrim tap, or the − control. It can never stand on top of a stave.

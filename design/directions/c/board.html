The Woodshed

Quiet craft studio. "Woodshedding" is what drummers call real practice — this direction is the town's shared practice room with the door open: stone, felt, walnut, brass hardware, graph paper. Newsreader for prose, IBM Plex for the workshop labels, hairline rules, ledger tables with dotted leaders. Light-first with an "after hours" dark theme. One CSS file, Eleventy, zero client dependencies.

The Single Paradiddle

The most useful sticking pattern ever invented.

15–20 min·Focus Rudiment & sticking·Tempo ♩ 70 → 100

Check these first: The Single Stroke Roll · The Double Stroke Roll

EX 1A
Single Paradiddle on Snare
4/4 · 16th notes · ♩ 70 → 100
Electronic ▾

Sticking: R L R R · L R L L. The accents fall on counts 1, 2, 3, 4 — the start of each group. Let those be slightly louder than the rest.

Move on when

  • RLRR LRLL × 2 at ♩=80 for 2 minutes without sticking errors
  • Accents on 1, 2, 3, 4 are clearly louder than the rest
